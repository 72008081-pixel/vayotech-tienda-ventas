## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges the frantic, gamified deal-seeking energy of cross-border value marketplaces (AliExpress, Temu) with the precision engineering, dark elegance, and tactile clarity of high-end audiophile hardware (Hi-Fi monitors, DACs, studio equipment). It serves tech-savvy shoppers and bargain hunters seeking premium audio, electronics, and imported novelties without sacrificing trust or visual polish.

The emotional response balances the adrenaline of discovery and urgent discount signals ("Liquidador Cusco", "Stock Limitado", wholesale tiers) with absolute confidence in transaction security and technical fidelity. The aesthetic is **Cyber-Electric Modernism**: deep void backgrounds, luminescent chromatic accents (blazing orange, electric purple, neon cyan), tactile audio-rack toggle cues, crisp geometric typography, and hyper-responsive micro-interactions.

## Colors

The palette operates in a native high-fidelity dark mode designed to elevate vibrant spectral accents without visual fatigue:

- **Primary (`#FF5E00`):** Electric Hyper-Orange. Reserved for conversion drivers, immediate checkout triggers, primary badges, and high-impact flash-sale counters.
- **Secondary (`#7928CA`):** Deep Electric Violet. Used for premium tiering, subtle neon glow underlays, and linear gradient transitions into Cyan.
- **Tertiary (`#00DFD8`):** Cyber Cyan. Encodes technical specs, frequency curves, Hi-Fi hardware details, and secondary interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#040711`):** Hi-Fi Onyx/Obsidian. Grounding space simulating anodized aluminum chassis and dark studio monitors.
- **Deal Emerald (`#10B981`):** Applied exclusively to monetary savings ("Ahorras S/.45"), wholesale discounts ("5+ Precio x Mayor"), and zero-fee badges ("Envío S/.3").
- **WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Dedicated floating support and instant Peruvian commerce closing trigger.

Use multi-stop gradient blends (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FF5E00 0%, #7928CA 60%, #00DFD8 100%)`) sparingly—exclusively for hero banners, active checkout CTAs, and promotional ribbon highlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct roles:
1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Delivers geometric precision, architectural impact, and modern industrial aesthetics. Numbers in pricing blocks adopt tabular sizing for dynamic count-ups.
2. **Outfit (Body & Product Details):** Delivers ultra-clean legibility at high reading speeds, balanced optical spacing, and neutral delivery for product descriptions, reviews, and specifications.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Badges, Codes, SKUs, Technical Specs):** Grounds the e-commerce urgency with a technical, high-precision instrument aesthetic. All micro-badges ("LIQUIDADOR", "STOCK: 3 UNID", "THD < 0.001%") rely on this monospaced style to evoke studio hardware readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected around a fluid responsive grid designed for dense product discovery:
- **Mobile (under 768px):** 2-column tight product grid with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 0.75rem` to maximize screen density reminiscent of mobile flash-sale apps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 3-column to 4-column reflow with `1rem` gutters.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column core shell, breaking into 4 or 5-column product catalogs with a fixed max-width container at `1280px` centered canvas.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px modular baseline (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Product cards enforce tight inner padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure critical commercial metadata (price, badge, shipping tag, rating) remains visible above the standard fold.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages high-tech glassmorphism and luminescent rim lighting rather than muddy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep matte `#040711`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Feed Panels):** Solid `#0A0F1E` bordered with a 1px hairline stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover transitions invoke an interactive outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(0, 223, 216, 0.12), inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 223, 216, 0.3)`.
- **Level 2 (Overlays, Floating Controls, Badges):** 12px backdrop blur with translucent dark backing `rgba(10, 15, 30, 0.75)` and sharp highlight rims (`rgba(255, 94, 0, 0.2)` on high-urgency modules).
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Instant Drawer):** Heavy frosted glass blur (24px) over `#040711` at 85% opacity, flanked by an ultra-thin gradient hairline boundary (`#FF5E00` through `#7928CA`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature:
- **Cards, Drawers, and Containers:** Bound by `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base, `1rem` on desktop cards, and `1.5rem` for bottom sheets and cart drawers) to soften technical rigidity and preserve touch-friendly friendliness.
- **Deal Badges & Micro-tags:** Styled as ultra-dense chamfered pills or tight soft lozenges (`rounded: 0.25rem` to `0.375rem`) to maintain industrial label integrity.
- **Floating Action Buttons (WhatsApp & Quick Cart Trigger):** Fully rounded circular/pill silhouettes for effortless thumb ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Buy Now / Agregar al Carrito):** High-energy gradient background (`#FF5E00` to `#FF3E00`) with white bold text, subtle inner top glow, and an active scale down effect (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary Action (Hi-Fi Specs / Comparar):** Translucent dark base with 1px border `#00DFD8` and text in `#00DFD8`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Purchase Trigger:** Fixed floating thumb-level button (right corner, bottom: 24px) using `#25D366` with pulsing neon radar animation, displaying instant message preview: "¿Dudas? Chatea al instante".

### Urgency & Value Badges (Temu / Flash-Sale Inspired)
- **"Liquidador Cusco":** High-contrast badge with `#FF5E00` background, black JetBrains Mono text, and flame micro-icon.
- **"Stock Limitado":** Amber-gold border (`#F59E0B`), dark translucent pill with blinking amber status dot.
- **"5+ Precio x Mayor":** Cyber Violet (`#7928CA`) background, white text, indicating tiered scale savings.
- **"Envío S/.3":** Bright Emerald pill (`#10B981`) with inverted bold badge typography.

### Product Card
- Encased in `#0A0F1E` with a 1px border (`#1E293B`).
- Square 1:1 image zone with aspect preservation, equipped with a top-left dynamic badge stack and top-right wishlist/audio-sample micro-button.
- Real-time stock depletion progress meter: slim 3px dual-color bar showing sales velocity.
- Pricing block layout: strikethrough retail price in muted slate (`#94A3B8`), followed by oversized Space Grotesk current price in high-voltage orange.

### Instant Cart Drawer
- Right-anchored slide-in sheet (420px desktop width, 100vw mobile width) triggered immediately upon item addition without page refresh.
- Includes a free shipping / discounted shipping progress bar animating dynamically toward the target threshold.
- Direct checkout button featuring 1-click Express and WhatsApp order confirmation.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Dark slate fill `#070B18`, 1px border `#1E293B`, focusing into vibrant cyan border `#00DFD8` with `0 0 12px rgba(0, 223, 216, 0.2)` bloom.
- Quantity selectors mimic Hi-Fi hardware stepping knobs/incrementers with tactile press feedback.
